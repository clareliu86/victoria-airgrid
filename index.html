Rebalancing

DJI data: 99.5 Wh battery · 200 g maximum payload · 100 W hub (≈ 60 min to 90 %, ≈ 78 min to full). Parcel mass, spare count, hub count and swap time are study assumptions. Run: 120 min.

Advanced

Rules: each order needs enough installed battery for the trip plus a 20 % study reserve, then books a conflict-free 4D slot. After landing and 30 s unloading, the used battery is removed and queued at that pier; the aircraft waits for a charged spare and a swap. Each hub charges one battery at a time; 90 % and 100 % targets use the DJI time points with a piecewise linear interpolation between them. No in-aircraft charging is assumed.
None – UAVs only move with an order.
Reactive – when a pier has waiting orders and no UAV is on the way, the nearest pier with a spare UAV sends one empty.
Proactive – piers with fewer ready UAVs than their target receive empty flights from piers above target.
